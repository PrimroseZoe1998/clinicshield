# ClinicShield Framework Reading Notes

Reading completed October 13 2026.

## Sources reviewed

- HICP 2023 Technical Volume 1, including all ten practices,
  introduction, and remaining material.
- HHS Cybersecurity Performance Goals, Purpose,
  Essential Goals, and Enhanced Goals.
- NIST SP 800-66 Rev. 2, Sections 3–5.
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0, Section 2 and Appendix A.

## Implications for ClinicShield

- Inventory and checklist findings support a broader risk analysis.
  The Cyber Safety Score does not certify compliance.
- Findings need evidence, practical actions, responsible owners,
  and a way to verify remediation.
- Individual accounts, prompt access removal, and limited privileges
  are related but distinct safeguards.
- A VPN alone does not establish multifactor authentication.
- Device encryption and transmission encryption protect different
  situations and need distinct evidence.
- Backups need protection and tested recovery.
- Security responsibility and documented risk analysis need
  separate follow-up answers.
- HHS performance goals are voluntary. Essential mappings must
  be checked before assigning extra scoring weight.
- Framework references describe relationships; one Yes answer
  does not prove that a whole framework outcome is satisfied.
